Privacy Policy

Lost & Soldered · Effective October 11, 2026

Lost & Soldered is a single-player game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app keeps

Your progress (finished repairs, clues, settings and any repair left half-done) is saved in a file on your device. It never leaves your device, apart from the device backups (iCloud or computer) that you control. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; the app only learns which packs your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
