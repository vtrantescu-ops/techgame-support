Support

Stuck on a repair, found a bug, or a purchase didn't arrive? Write to vt_apps@yahoo.com with your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you can.

I'm stuck on a repair

Tap the lightbulb on the bench: it shows the next step. The clipboard opens the work order. With the Meter selected, Service manual lists what a healthy unit should read.

Tracing circles on screws is hard

Settings (the gear in the workshop) → Hold to turn screws. A screw then turns while you hold a finger on it.

I keep breaking things

Settings → Relaxed repairs. Rough handling can't snap clips, strip screws or lift pads. Diagnosis mistakes still count.

My pack is missing

Open the shop from the workshop (or Settings) and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Packs are one-time purchases and stay yours.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
